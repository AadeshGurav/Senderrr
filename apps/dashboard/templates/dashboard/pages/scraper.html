{# ── How it works ──────────────────────────────────────────────────── #}
How the scraper works
  1. Every 5 minutes the scraper fetches SCRAPER_TARGET_URLS.
  2. It compares article URLs against the ArticleHash table.
  3. First run: all current articles are "seeded" as known so old articles aren't broadcast. Only articles published after the seed will be sent automatically.
  4. New articles are stored and a broadcast fan-out is triggered to all active groups.
  5. Articles older than SCRAPER_MAX_ARTICLE_AGE_HOURS ({{ max_age_hours|default:"12" }}h) are skipped.

If nothing is broadcasting: use "Unseed & Broadcast" to immediately push existing articles to groups as a smoke test.
